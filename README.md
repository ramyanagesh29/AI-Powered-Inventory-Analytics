
# AI-Powered Inventory Analytics & Demand Prediction

## IBM SkillsBuild Data Analytics with AI Internship 2026

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail inventory and demand data to identify demand
patterns, potential inventory risks, product-level trends, and factors
associated with demand.

A Random Forest regression model is also developed to estimate demand using
historical inventory, pricing, promotion, seasonal, weather, and other
available features.

The project follows a data analytics workflow from data preparation and
exploratory analysis to business insights, machine learning, and
recommendations.

---

## Problem Statement

Retail businesses need to maintain sufficient inventory while responding to
changing customer demand. Poor inventory planning can result in potential
inventory shortages or inefficient stock allocation.

This project uses data analytics and machine learning to understand demand
patterns, identify potential inventory-risk conditions, and support
demand-based inventory planning.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze retail demand and inventory patterns.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Identify potential inventory-risk areas.
- Analyze demand across categories and regions.
- Analyze the relationship between demand and promotion, discount, seasonality,
  weather, and pricing-related variables.
- Identify high-demand and high-risk products.
- Build a machine learning model for demand prediction.
- Evaluate and compare machine learning models.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains:

- 76,000 records
- 16 original columns
- Date range: January 2022 to January 2024

- Category

- Inventory Level
- Units Sold

- Promotion
